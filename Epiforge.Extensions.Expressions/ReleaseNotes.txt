ObserveCast and ObserveOfType no longer copy their results each time they are enumerated after a change. Since 7.1.5, every change discarded the copy they enumerate and the next enumeration made a new one, so an aggregate over either, which reads every element after every change, copied the whole sequence for each change it followed. An enumeration now reads the results where they are, and a change copies them only while an enumeration begun before it is still under way, so it goes on reading them as they were.
