`ObserveValueFor` and `ObserveValueForOrDefault` follow a change announced under a key the dictionary's comparer considers equal to the one observed, which is how a case-insensitive dictionary announces a change made through a differently cased key; they missed it before.
A query whose fault stands for the same reason through a change to its source keeps reporting the same fault and announces nothing, where it replaced the fault with a new one and announced it: an element or single element out of range, the extreme or average of an empty sequence, the value for a missing key, the rank of an element not ordered, the faults of a projection over a collection, and the null and duplicate keys of a projection over a dictionary.
