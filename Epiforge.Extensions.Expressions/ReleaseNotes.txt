Observing an expression with direct subscription no longer throws NotSupportedException from the expression compiler when a property of a value type other than a primitive, such as TimeSpan.Ticks, or an operator whose method takes its operands by reference, is applied to an operand containing a subexpression the observer holds (a call or construction whose value it disposes of, or a property whose change notifications are ignored, when nothing it is made from can change) and that operand is evaluated after another; such an operand is now read from a temporary, which is what the graph does in effect, so the observation produces a value or a fault as the graph does and still takes the fast path.
