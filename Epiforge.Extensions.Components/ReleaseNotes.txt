FastInvoker, the FastInvoke and FastDefault extensions, FastComparer and FastEqualityComparer now work where the runtime cannot generate code, as under Native AOT and on iOS: there they invoke and compare through reflection rather than through generated methods, with the same results, including comparisons which honor a type's own IComparable<T> or IEquatable<T> as the default comparers do, and exceptions which arrive unwrapped.
The package is marked as compatible with ahead-of-time compilation, and the members which need generated code or reflect over what trimming may remove now say so: ExceptionExtensions.GetFullDetails, ReflectionExtensions.GetImplementationEvents, GetImplementationMethods and GetImplementationProperties, DynamicPropertyChangeNotifier, DynamicDisposable, DynamicAsyncDisposable and DynamicSyncDisposable.
IsDisposed on the disposable base classes now reads with acquire semantics, so a thread waiting on another thread's disposal sees it; before, a loop polling IsDisposed could be compiled to read the field once and never finish, as it did on .NET 10 on Apple Silicon and on Linux.
