Breaking: Dispose and DisposeAsync are no longer virtual on the disposal base classes, which now own the disposal claim, the events, and the suppression of finalization themselves; the extension point is the protected Dispose(bool) and DisposeAsyncCore methods, the latter of which replaces DisposeAsync(bool). The Disposed, Disposing, and DisposalOverridden events are now declared by INotifyDisposed, INotifyDisposing, and INotifyDisposalOverridden, and DisposalNotificationEventArgs has been removed in favor of EventArgs.
DisposeAsyncCore is virtual on Disposable and DynamicDisposable and defaults to the synchronous Dispose(bool), so a type whose cleanup is entirely synchronous implements one method rather than two. It remains abstract on AsyncDisposable and DynamicAsyncDisposable, which have no synchronous path to delegate to.
