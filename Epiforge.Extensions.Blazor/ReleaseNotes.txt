Epiforge.Extensions.Blazor 1.0.0 is the first release: reactive Blazor components with an API modeled on Phork.Blazor.Reactivity, in which ReactiveComponentBase and ReactiveLayoutComponentBase re-render when an object along what Observed or ObservedCollection read announces a change, release what a render did not use when it ends, compile nothing per call or per component, render each component once per turn of its dispatcher and the components of one renderer in one batch, and reproduce the lifecycle of ComponentBase without reflection, so that they work under Native AOT. Re-rendering a table of 1,000 cells which observe what they observed takes 603.1 μs against Phork.Blazor.Reactivity 1.1.3's 191,914.2 μs and allocates nothing beyond the expression trees the compiler builds for each call, a hundred rows of ten cells entering and leaving take 1,333.5 μs against 507,745.0 μs, and 1,000 cells changed together render once each, in one batch, in 897.4 μs against Phork's 2,000 batches in 293,613.8 μs.
