Inserting, removing, replacing and moving a range in an observable range collection which announces once per operation, and removing each run of matches, now copies and shifts the collection once rather than once for each element, so an operation on n elements near the start of a collection of length m costs time proportional to m rather than to n times m: removing the front half of 16,000 elements now takes 9.45 μs where it took 75.0 ms, moving it to the end 11.1 μs where it took 74.8 ms, and inserting 8,000 at the start 12.5 μs where it took 6.06 ms, and no range operation allocates more than it did, a move half. A range operation given a range which does not fit the collection now throws before changing anything rather than after changing part of it.
