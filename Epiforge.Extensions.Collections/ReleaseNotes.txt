PrefixWeightedSequence gained NodeAtFrom and NodeAtWeightFrom, which search outward from a node you already hold rather than from the root. The cost is the logarithm of the distance between the node you have and the position you want rather than the logarithm of the whole sequence, and on the weight-based variant a run of zero-weight items is climbed over rather than walked. Each decides per call whether starting from the node is actually cheaper, and starts from the root when it is not.
