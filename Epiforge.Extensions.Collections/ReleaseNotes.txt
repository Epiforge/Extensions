Breaking: the read-only wrappers no longer implement or accept the mutating interfaces they wrap. ReadOnlyRangeDictionary, ReadOnlyObservableRangeCollection, and ReadOnlyObservableRangeDictionary now take and implement the corresponding IReadOnly interfaces, so a read-only view can no longer be passed to something that expects to change it. ValueComparisonUnequalException has been removed.
Added PrefixWeightedSequence and PrefixWeightedSequenceNode, a sequence in which every position carries a weight, and in which insertion, removal, movement, changing a weight, and finding a position by index or by accumulated weight are all logarithmic in the number of positions. A node is a durable handle to a position, so you can hold one instead of re-finding an index after every change.
